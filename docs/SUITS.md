# The nine suits: one per stream of the Hundred Schools (九流)

Owner, 23 Nov 18:58-19:00: "redo the 10 甲 as they are base of 百家争鸣; in a way Kaymuse is the Qin emperor." Owner, 23 Nov 19:57: go for the suits as one per school; 20:03: nine schools in the match, 稗甲 dropped; clean the GDD of outside influences.
Status: APPROVED DESIGN (owner 19:57). Kits are built in batch 16 (法甲 sample, owner plays and approves) and batch 17 (the other eight). Numbers go in CONFIG at build time.

## Why these nine
The Han court catalogue (《汉书·艺文志》) lists 九流十家: ten schools, of which it counts only nine as worth reading (可观者九家而已): 儒、道、阴阳、法、名、墨、纵横、杂、农. These nine are the suits in the match (owner 23 Nov 20:03: "start with the 9 schools since that is accurate"). The tenth, the tales school 小说家 (稗甲), is not counted by the court and is not summoned by Kaymuse; its design is kept at the end for reference only.

## Kaymuse as the first-emperor figure (never named, never quoted)
Tone rule kept: no real ruler is named or quoted. She is the archetype, shown through what she does:
- **One answer for all schools.** She ends the Hundred Schools' debate by decree. That is the unifier's move: one script, one axle width, one law (书同文，车同轨).
- **The royale is her burning of the books.** Eight of the nine schools' suits are broken in her palace; only one doctrine may remain. Her collapses fall on the schools' own halls.
- **朕 ("We").** Already her voice. Historically the first emperor made 朕 the ruler's own word.
- **Water and black.** The first emperor's court claimed the Water virtue (水德) and the colour black. Her dais, banners and the Tiger Demon's cracks glow are black and water-blue at the art stage.
- **Her decrees are law (法), enforced by the two handles (二柄): reward and punishment.** The bounty is the reward handle (赏); the slow and mark for breaking a decree is the punishment handle (罚). The systems already built get their names, and no new system is added.
- **The twist still holds.** Even the surviving doctrine ends as the throne's beast: the winner becomes the next tiger statue.

Dread stays aimed at her rules and her palace. No school's teaching is mocked; each suit fights for its doctrine.

## Panel votes (2 jam judges, 2 players, 2 critics, design and tech leads; majority followed)
1. **Doctrine titles for every suit: 7-0.** Each title comes from its school's own teaching or office, so every system, kit and name is new.
2. **Kaymuse as the unnamed first-emperor archetype, with decrees framed as law and two handles: 7-0 / 6-1.** The critic against the framing asked that the two-handle names appear only in her voice lines and the GDD; the HUD keeps plain words.
3. **Every kit's signature move plays with a royale system already built (decrees, plates, the mark, bots, collapses, hiding): 6-1.** Kits are then not interchangeable MOBA templates. Against (tech lead): more cross-system tests. Accepted, with one check per signature.
4. **Roles from the GDD table kept, except 道甲, which moves from Tank to Evasive duelist: 5-2.** Yielding and water do not fit soaking damage. 墨甲 stays the tank and keeps no dash.
5. **First kit sample: 法甲 (4), 儒甲 (2), 纵横甲 (1).** 法甲 is the empress's own doctrine and interacts with decrees and the mark, so it shows the theme fastest.
6. **Owner fixes (19:57, the lead's two recommendations taken):** 农甲's sprouts heal and feed 农甲 only (as the GDD said); 杂甲 gets a dash of its own.

### 儒甲 · 君子 (Confucian; kit = 君子六艺, owner 23 Nov 20:08-20:12)
Source: 《周礼·地官·保氏》「乃教之六艺：一曰五礼，二曰六乐，三曰五射，四曰五驭，五曰六书，六曰九数」. Six arts in five slots; 射 and 数 share the attack.
- Passive **礼 · 君子之争** (「君子无所争，必也射乎……其争也君子」): bonus damage to the suit that last hit him; less damage taken while only one enemy is near (a fair duel).
- Attack **射 + 数, range-switching (owner 20:12)**: the basic attack changes with distance, no button. Close (target within about 2.5 m): **佩剑**, the gentleman's sword, a melee cut. Mid (out to about 6 m): **五射**, an arrow. **数**: every 6th hit of either kind is the strong one: 参连 (a three-arrow volley) at mid, a heavy cut that pushes back at close.
- Q **御 · 五驭 · 逐禽左** (dash): chariot charge about 5 m; a suit it hits is driven to the side.
- W **书 · 六书 · 正名**: brush-seal shot; the target's plates and boons stop working for a few seconds.
- R **乐 · 六乐 · 编钟**: bronze bells ring three times around him over about 2 s, each ring shown on the ground first; each ring damages and slows, the third stuns briefly; every suit hit heals him (仁).
- Panel (23 Nov 20:08): 射 as the attack 5-2 (then the owner's close/mid switch answered the players' melee wish); 数 as the 6th hit 6-1; 书·正名 strips plates and boons 7-0; 乐 bells with a full tell 7-0.
- Plays with: plates and the leader (正名), the bounty, duels under the hunter cap.

### 道甲 · 真人 (Daoist; kit from 《道德经》, owner 23 Nov 20:15: both skills are counters, the ultimate multiplies his base stats by the counters made since the last ultimate, reset after use)
- Passive **无为** (「道常无为而无不为」, ch. 37): standing still and not attacking for 2 s hides him (shimmer within about 3 m). Silk curtains hide every suit.
- Attack **水**: open-hand strike, melee.
- Q **柔 · 柔弱胜刚强** (ch. 36), counter to basic attacks: a short stance (about 0.8 s). The first basic attack that would hit him in it does no damage and the attacker is pushed back and takes it instead. Success: +1 counter, half the cooldown back.
- W **不争 · 夫唯不争，故天下莫能与之争** (ch. 22), counter to skills: a short stance (about 0.8 s). The first enemy skill that would hit him misses him: he flows like water to a spot beside the caster (his only dash, and only on success), and the skill's slow or stun does not land. Success: +1 counter, half the cooldown back.
- R **反 · 反者道之动，弱者道之用** (ch. 40): for about 8 s his base damage, speed and damage resistance are multiplied by 1 + (counters x step). Counters reset to 0 after use. Panel (20:15): step about 8% per counter, at most 5 counted, so the top is 1.4x, the game-wide bonus cap; the count shows on the R button. With 0 counters the ultimate gives nothing, so the player learns to counter first.
- Fairness: each stance shows a water shimmer that every suit can see (0.8 s, above the 0.4 s tell rule), so attackers can hold off. Counters count against suits, cubs, minions and the Tiger Demon (claw = basic attack; pounce, roar and the stolen moves = skills); the statue, lions and collapses are not fighters and cannot be countered.
- Plays with: the apex hunt (countering the demon builds the ultimate), hiding, duels under the hunter cap.

### 法甲 · 廷尉 (Legalist: law 法, method 术, authority 势, the two handles 二柄) - first sample
- Passive **Law Binds All**: bonus damage to marked suits, i.e. anyone who broke a decree. This suit believes the law binds even the throne; she does not favour it.
- Attack: bronze sword, melee.
- Q **Pursuit**: dash, longer against a marked or low-health target.
- W **Two Handles**: at a target, punishment (slow). On yourself, reward (speed). Same button; aim decides.
- R **Sentence**: a delayed strike shown on the ground; damage grows with the target's missing health.
- Plays with: decrees, the mark and the bounty.

### 墨甲 · 墨守 (Mohist: universal care 兼爱, against aggression 非攻, defence craft)
- Passive **Against Aggression (非攻)**: stronger while holding ground, weaker while chasing far from where it last stood.
- Attack: crossbow bolt, mid range.
- Q **Rampart**: raise a short wall for a few seconds (blocks movement and bolts).
- W **Crossbow Engine**: place a turret for a few seconds (from a pool built once).
- R **Hold the City (守城)**: a circle where you take less damage and incoming projectiles stop at its edge.
- No dash.
- Plays with: collapses and chokepoints such as bridges and gates.

### 阴阳甲 · 太极 (Yin-yang; frame 阴阳 only, owner 23 Nov 20:19: the ultimate is locked; he must cycle through yin and yang to unlock 太极)
- Passive **阴阳 (the lock)**: a taiji symbol on his HUD and R button. Landing a 阴 skill fills the dark half; landing a 阳 skill fills the light half. A half that is already full gains nothing, so he must alternate. Both halves full = 太极 unlocked. After 太极 both halves empty again. For this suit the lock replaces ultimate charge; under the "Ultimates 2x" decree only the cooldown after use runs 2x, the lock still needs both halves.
- Attack **两仪**: long-range orb that alternates dark and light each shot (look only; basic attacks do not fill the halves, panel 20:19).
- Q **阴 · 月** (yin: moon, cold, drawing in): a slow long-range orb; on hit it slows the target and drains a little health to him. Casting it pushes him a short step back (yin withdraws): his only movement skill. Fills the dark half.
- W **阳 · 日** (yang: sun, heat, going out): a sunburst at a point, shown on the ground first, then a burst that burns. Fills the light half.
- R **太极** (locked until both halves are full): a great taiji disk at long range, shown in full first. Its dark half pulls inward and slows; its light half burns. After about 1.5 s it turns half a circle, so every suit inside takes both halves. Then the lock resets.
- Panel (20:19): only skills fill the halves (basic attacks would unlock it too fast) 6-1; a backstep inside 阴 instead of a separate dash 5-2; full ground tells on 阳 and 太极 7-0.
- Plays with: the "Ultimates 2x" decree (cooldown only), bridges silence (cannot fill halves there), long sightlines from the heights.

### 杂甲 · 门客 (Syncretist: borrows from every school; its great book was compiled by a chancellor's retainers in the empress-figure's own court)
- Passive **Many Masters**: keeps the last enemy skill that hit it as a stored copy.
- Attack: mixed bolt, mid range.
- Q **Borrowed Doctrine**: cast the stored copy, weaker.
- W **Borrowed Road**: dash up to 5 m back along the line of the last enemy skill that hit it (toward where it came from). No other dash works this way.
- R **Encyclopaedia**: for a few seconds, every hit taken stores its skill and casts it back.
- Plays with: every other kit.

### 名甲 · 辩者 (School of Names: "a white horse is not a horse", the paradoxes)
- Owner decision kept: attacks with the sword.
- Passive **Hard and White Apart (离坚白)**: hits from range and hits up close are counted apart; alternating them adds damage.
- Attack: thrown flying sword, long range.
- Q **White Horse**: blink. A sword stays where you stood and strikes after a moment.
- W **Flying Arrow at Rest** (from the debaters' paradox that a flying arrowhead has a moment when it neither moves nor stops): swords hang in the air in a line, then all fly.
- R **Hundred Paradoxes**: a ring of hanging swords around a point that fires inward after a full tell.
- Plays with: tells and dodging (every hanging sword is a visible tell).

### 纵横甲 · 说客 (Diplomatists: the vertical alliance 合纵 against the strong state, and the horizontal alliance 连横 that served it)
- GDD decision kept: links two enemies so they share damage.
- Passive **Persuasion**: bots that see this suit prefer another target for a moment.
- Attack: tally token, mid range.
- Q **Vertical Alliance (合纵)**: tether two enemies; damage to one is shared with the other.
- W **Horizontal Alliance (连横)**: swap places with a target (the dash).
- R **Sow Discord**: enemies in an area deal more damage to each other and less to you for a few seconds.
- Plays with: bots, the leader and the bounty.

### 农甲 · 耕者 (Agriculturalists: ruler and people till the fields together 并耕)
- GDD decision kept: plants heal or feed it, harvest ultimate.
- Passive **Till Together**: heals more the lower its health.
- Attack: hoe, melee.
- Q **Sow**: seeds that grow over a few seconds into sprouts that only 农甲 can eat: walking over one heals and feeds it (a short damage boost).
- W **Plough Charge**: dash that leaves a furrow that slows.
- R **Harvest**: eat every grown sprout you planted for healing and a burst.
- Plays with: altars and the "Altars are dry" decree (its sprouts still work when altars are dry).
