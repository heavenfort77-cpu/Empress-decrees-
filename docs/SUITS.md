# The nine suits: one per stream of the Hundred Schools (九流)

Owner, 23 Nov 18:58-19:00: "redo the 10 甲 as they are base of 百家争鸣; in a way Kaymuse is the Qin emperor." Owner, 23 Nov 19:57: go for the suits as one per school; 20:03: nine schools in the match, 稗甲 dropped; clean the GDD of outside influences.
Status: APPROVED DESIGN (owner 19:57). Kits are built in batch 16 (法甲 sample, owner plays and approves) and batch 17 (the other eight). Numbers go in CONFIG at build time.

## Why these nine
The Han court catalogue (《汉书·艺文志》) lists 九流十家: ten schools, of which it counts only nine as worth reading (可观者九家而已): 儒、道、阴阳、法、名、墨、纵横、杂、农. These nine are the suits in the match (owner 23 Nov 20:03: "start with the 9 schools since that is accurate"). The tenth, the tales school 小说家 (稗甲), is not counted by the court and is not summoned by Kaymuse; its design is kept at the end for reference only.

## Kaymuse as the first-emperor figure (never named, never quoted)
Tone rule kept: no real ruler is named or quoted. She is the archetype, shown through what she does:
- **One answer for all schools.** She ends the Hundred Schools' debate by decree. That is the unifier's move: one script, one axle width, one law (书同文，车同轨).
- **The royale is her burning of the books.** Eight of the nine schools' suits are broken in her palace; only one doctrine may remain. Her collapses fall on the schools' own halls.
- **朕 ("We").** Already her voice. Historically the first emperor made 朕 the ruler's own word.
- **Water and black.** The first emperor's court claimed the Water virtue (水德) and the colour black. Her dais, banners and the Tiger Demon's cracks glow are black and water-blue at the art stage.
- **Her decrees are law (法), enforced by the two handles (二柄): reward and punishment.** The bounty is the reward handle (赏); the slow and mark for breaking a decree is the punishment handle (罚). The systems already built get their names, and no new system is added.
- **The twist still holds.** Even the surviving doctrine ends as the throne's beast: the winner becomes the next tiger statue.

Dread stays aimed at her rules and her palace. No school's teaching is mocked; each suit fights for its doctrine.

## Panel votes (2 jam judges, 2 players, 2 critics, design and tech leads; majority followed)
1. **Doctrine titles for every suit: 7-0.** Each title comes from its school's own teaching or office, so every system, kit and name is new.
2. **Kaymuse as the unnamed first-emperor archetype, with decrees framed as law and two handles: 7-0 / 6-1.** The critic against the framing asked that the two-handle names appear only in her voice lines and the GDD; the HUD keeps plain words.
3. **Every kit's signature move plays with a royale system already built (decrees, plates, the mark, bots, collapses, hiding): 6-1.** Kits are then not interchangeable MOBA templates. Against (tech lead): more cross-system tests. Accepted, with one check per signature.
4. **Roles from the GDD table kept, except 道甲, which moves from Tank to Evasive duelist: 5-2.** Yielding and water do not fit soaking damage. 墨甲 stays the tank and keeps no dash.
5. **First kit sample: 法甲 (4), 儒甲 (2), 纵横甲 (1).** 法甲 is the empress's own doctrine and interacts with decrees and the mark, so it shows the theme fastest.
6. **Owner fixes (19:57, the lead's two recommendations taken):** 农甲's sprouts heal and feed 农甲 only (as the GDD said); 杂甲 gets a dash of its own.

### 儒甲 · 君子 (Confucian; kit = 君子六艺, owner 23 Nov 20:08-20:12)
Source: 《周礼·地官·保氏》「乃教之六艺：一曰五礼，二曰六乐，三曰五射，四曰五驭，五曰六书，六曰九数」. Six arts in five slots; 射 and 数 share the attack.
- Passive **礼 · 君子之争** (「君子无所争，必也射乎……其争也君子」): bonus damage to the suit that last hit him; less damage taken while only one enemy is near (a fair duel).
- Attack **射 + 数, range-switching (owner 20:12)**: the basic attack changes with distance, no button. Close (target within about 2.5 m): **佩剑**, the gentleman's sword, a melee cut. Mid (out to about 6 m): **五射**, an arrow. **数**: every 6th hit of either kind is the strong one: 参连 (a three-arrow volley) at mid, a heavy cut that pushes back at close.
- Q **御 · 五驭 · 逐禽左** (dash): chariot charge about 5 m; a suit it hits is driven to the side.
- W **书 · 六书 · 正名**: brush-seal shot; the target's plates and boons stop working for a few seconds.
- R **乐 · 六乐 · 编钟**: bronze bells ring three times around him over about 2 s, each ring shown on the ground first; each ring damages and slows, the third stuns briefly; every suit hit heals him (仁).
- Panel (23 Nov 20:08): 射 as the attack 5-2 (then the owner's close/mid switch answered the players' melee wish); 数 as the 6th hit 6-1; 书·正名 strips plates and boons 7-0; 乐 bells with a full tell 7-0.
- Plays with: plates and the leader (正名), the bounty, duels under the hunter cap.

### 道甲 · 真人 (Daoist; kit from 《道德经》, owner 23 Nov 20:15: both skills are counters, the ultimate multiplies his base stats by the counters made since the last ultimate, reset after use)
- Passive **无为** (「道常无为而无不为」, ch. 37): standing still and not attacking for 2 s hides him (shimmer within about 3 m). Silk curtains hide every suit.
- Attack **水**: open-hand strike, melee.
- Q **柔 · 柔弱胜刚强** (ch. 36), counter to basic attacks: a short stance (about 0.8 s). The first basic attack that would hit him in it does no damage and the attacker is pushed back and takes it instead. Success: +1 counter, half the cooldown back.
- W **不争 · 夫唯不争，故天下莫能与之争** (ch. 22), counter to skills: a short stance (about 0.8 s). The first enemy skill that would hit him misses him: he flows like water to a spot beside the caster (his only dash, and only on success), and the skill's slow or stun does not land. Success: +1 counter, half the cooldown back.
- R **反 · 反者道之动，弱者道之用** (ch. 40): for about 8 s his base damage, speed and damage resistance are multiplied by 1 + (counters x step). Counters reset to 0 after use. Panel (20:15): step about 8% per counter, at most 5 counted, so the top is 1.4x, the game-wide bonus cap; the count shows on the R button. With 0 counters the ultimate gives nothing, so the player learns to counter first.
- Fairness: each stance shows a water shimmer that every suit can see (0.8 s, above the 0.4 s tell rule), so attackers can hold off. Counters count against suits, cubs, minions and the Tiger Demon (claw = basic attack; pounce, roar and the stolen moves = skills); the statue, lions and collapses are not fighters and cannot be countered.
- Plays with: the apex hunt (countering the demon builds the ultimate), hiding, duels under the hunter cap.

### 法甲 · 廷尉 (Legalist; frame 法术势, owner 23 Nov 20:22; first kit sample)
- Passive **法 · 法不阿贵** (「法不阿贵，绳不挠曲」, 韩非子·有度): the law favours no one, the highest least of all. Bonus damage against the current leader and against marked suits (decree breakers); the two do not stack (the higher applies).
- Attack: bronze sword, melee.
- Q **法 · 刑无等级** (商鞅: punishment knows no rank), dash: pursuit to a target; longer against a marked suit, the leader or a target under half health.
- W **术 · 潜御** (「术者，藏之于胸中……而潜御群臣者也」, 韩非子·难三): unseen for about 2 s (shimmer within about 3 m, broken by attacking or being hit). His first hit from it exposes the target: it is seen by every suit for a few seconds.
- R **势 · 抱法处势** (「抱法处势则治」, 韩非子·难势): for about 6 s he holds authority: enemies within about 6 m are slowed and cannot dash (no escape from the law), and his hits grow with the target's missing health. Shown as a ring on the ground for its whole length.
- Panel (20:22): leader + marked passive, no stacking 6-1 (critic: it piles onto the bounty; answer: the higher applies, cap 1.4x); 术 as a short stealth 5-2 (players: stealth assassins frustrate; answer: 2 s, shimmer, breaks on a hit); 势 as lock-down plus execute 6-1.
- Plays with: decrees and the mark, the leader and bounty, fog (exposure), hiding.

### 墨甲 · 墨守 (Mohist; frames 墨守, 墨经, 非攻; owner 23 Nov 20:28). No dash (GDD rule).
- Passive **非攻** (against aggression): he does not start wars, he ends them. Bonus damage against any suit that attacked someone in the last 4 s; and he takes less damage while he has not moved for 1 s (holding the wall).
- Attack **连弩** (the repeating crossbow of the Mohist siege chapters): bolts, mid range.
- Q **墨守 · 距云梯** (turning back the siege ladders): raise a short wall at a point for a few seconds; it blocks movement and projectiles.
- W **墨经 · 衡** (the lever and balance of the Mohist canon): a lever plate at a point, shown for about 0.6 s, then it flings every suit on it a few metres away from him. Near a broken rail it can throw a suit into the void (knockback is the only way through those gaps).
- R **墨守 · 九距之** (「公输盘九设攻城之机变，子墨子九距之」): for about 8 s the next nine hits on him are turned back: no damage, and the attacker is pushed away. The HUD counts them down 9 to 0.
- Panel (20:28): the aggressor passive 6-1; the lever with a 0.6 s tell 6-1 (critic: a fling into the void can feel cheap; answer: shown first, only at broken-rail gaps); the nine defences 5-2 (players: close to 道甲's counters; answer: 道甲 times single counters to grow an ultimate, 墨甲 simply absorbs nine, no timing).
- Plays with: bridges and the gate (walls), broken rails (the lever), collapses, the hunter cap (aggressors come to him).

### 阴阳甲 · 太极 (Yin-yang; frame 阴阳 only, owner 23 Nov 20:19: the ultimate is locked; he must cycle through yin and yang to unlock 太极)
- Passive **阴阳 (the lock)**: a taiji symbol on his HUD and R button. Landing a 阴 skill fills the dark half; landing a 阳 skill fills the light half. A half that is already full gains nothing, so he must alternate. Both halves full = 太极 unlocked. After 太极 both halves empty again. For this suit the lock replaces ultimate charge; under the "Ultimates 2x" decree only the cooldown after use runs 2x, the lock still needs both halves.
- Attack **两仪**: long-range orb that alternates dark and light each shot (look only; basic attacks do not fill the halves, panel 20:19).
- Q **阴 · 月** (yin: moon, cold, drawing in): a slow long-range orb; on hit it slows the target and drains a little health to him. Casting it pushes him a short step back (yin withdraws): his only movement skill. Fills the dark half.
- W **阳 · 日** (yang: sun, heat, going out): a sunburst at a point, shown on the ground first, then a burst that burns. Fills the light half.
- R **太极** (locked until both halves are full): a great taiji disk at long range, shown in full first. Its dark half pulls inward and slows; its light half burns. After about 1.5 s it turns half a circle, so every suit inside takes both halves. Then the lock resets.
- Panel (20:19): only skills fill the halves (basic attacks would unlock it too fast) 6-1; a backstep inside 阴 instead of a separate dash 5-2; full ground tells on 阳 and 太极 7-0.
- Plays with: the "Ultimates 2x" decree (cooldown only), bridges silence (cannot fill halves there), long sightlines from the heights.

### 杂甲 · 门客 (Syncretist; frame 兼儒墨，合名法 from 《汉书·艺文志》, owner 23 Nov 20:32; panel picked it over 十二纪 5-2 because a month cycle repeats 阴阳甲's yin-yang cycle)
- Passive **兼收并蓄** (take the best of every school): stores the last enemy skill that hit him; its icon shows on the R button.
- Attack **议** (the counsellor's word): orb, mid range.
- Q **兼儒墨** (one cast, two schools): a seal shot. 儒 (正名): the target's plates and boons stop working for about 2 s (shorter than 儒甲's). 墨 (非攻): if the target attacked someone in the last 4 s, it is also slowed.
- W **合名法** (the dash, his own): a blink to a point (名: what you saw is not where he is). 法 (术): the first enemy within about 3 m of where he lands is exposed (seen by every suit) for about 3 s. Differs from 名甲's shadow blink and 法甲's pursuit.
- R **一字千金** (the book no one could change by a word): casts the stored enemy skill at full strength as his own, and resets his Q and W cooldowns.
- Panel (20:32): Q's two-school seal 6-1; W blink plus exposure 6-1; R full-strength copy plus reset 5-2 (critic: a copy must be readable; answer: the stored skill's icon sits on the R button and the cast keeps the original's tell and colour).
- Plays with: every other kit, the leader (strip), the mark and fog (exposure).
- Lore: his school's great book was compiled in the unifier's own court; he is the insider who knows every school's tricks, and says 「天下非一人之天下也」 to her face.

### 名甲 · 辩者 (School of Names; frame: the debaters' paradoxes, 辩者二十一事 in 《庄子·天下》; owner 23 Nov 20:25, sword rule removed)
- Passive **一尺之棰，日取其半，万世不竭** (the rod halved each day never runs out): each hit in a row on the same target adds half of the bonus still left, so the bonus climbs toward +40% (the game cap) and never quite reaches it; switching target starts again.
- Attack **轮不蹍地** (the wheel never touches the ground): a thrown bronze wheel, long range, that flies out and back (two chances to hit). Distinct from 儒甲's bow.
- Q **飞鸟之景未尝动也** (the flying bird's shadow never moves), dash: he blinks; his shadow stays where he stood for about 1 s, and bots and auto-target take it for him.
- W **镞矢之疾，而有不行不止之时** (the swift arrowhead has a moment when it neither moves nor stops): arrowheads hang in a line in the air, shown for about 1 s (anyone touching one is slowed), then all fly along the line.
- R **指不至，至不绝** (pointing never arrives; arriving never ends): he points along a very long line, shown for about 1 s, then a piercing strike runs its whole length; every suit it passes is hit.
- Panel (20:25): the wheel as the attack so 名甲 does not repeat 儒甲's bow 6-1; the halving passive capped at +40% 7-0; the shadow decoy 5-2 (players: decoys can feel cheap; answer: 1 s and only once per cast); full tells on W and R 7-0.
- Plays with: tells and dodging, bots (the shadow), long sightlines from the heights.

### 纵横甲 · 说客 (Diplomatists; frames 合纵, 连横, 不攻自破; owner 23 Nov 20:30). GDD rule kept: links two enemies so they share damage.
- Passive **连横 · 事一强** (each state allies with the strong one by one): a bot that sees him takes another target if one is in reach (once per bot every 6 s). The player is never steered.
- Attack **符节** (the envoy's tally): token shot, mid range.
- Q **合纵** (the vertical alliance), the link: a cord between two enemies within range for about 5 s; half of all damage to one is also dealt to the other. If they move more than about 8 m apart the cord snaps and both are slowed.
- W **连横** (the horizontal alliance), the dash: swap places with a suit or cub within range. It can put an enemy where he stood: into a tell, a warned section, a crowd.
- R **不攻自破** (it breaks without being attacked): a large circle, shown for its whole length (about 5 s). Bots inside turn on the nearest other suit (not the player beyond the hunter cap), and all damage suits inside deal to each other is raised. The player keeps control of their own suit.
- Panel (20:30): bot-only persuasion, never the player 6-1; the swap dash 7-0; 不攻自破 steering bots only, damage raised for everyone inside 5-2 (critic: hard to read; answer: the circle shows for its whole length and a HUD line names it).
- Plays with: bots, the hunter cap, the leader and bounty (link the leader to its hunter), tells and collapses (the swap).

### 农甲 · 耕者 (Agriculturalists; frames 播百谷, 并耕, 百草生机; owner 23 Nov 20:35). GDD rule kept: plants heal or feed it, harvest ultimate.
- Passive **并耕而食** (「贤者与民并耕而食」): walking over one of his grown sprouts eats it: it heals him and feeds him (a short damage boost). Only 农甲 can eat its sprouts; an enemy walking over one tramples it (counterplay).
- Attack **耒** (the plough-hoe): melee.
- Q **播百谷** (sow the hundred grains): scatter three seeds at a point; they grow into sprouts in about 4 s and last about 20 s (a fixed pool of 9).
- W **并耕** (the dash): a plough charge of about 5 m that leaves a furrow for about 4 s; enemies on it are slowed, and seeds on it grow in half the time.
- R **百草生机** (the life of the hundred herbs), the harvest: every seed he has planted ripens at once, and every ripe sprout within about 8 m is pulled to him and eaten; each one heals him and sends a pulse of damage around him. More sprouts, bigger harvest.
- Panel (20:35): enemies trample sprouts 6-1 (it gives a fight to a field); furrow speeds the seeds 7-0; the harvest pulls sprouts in 6-1.
- Plays with: altars and "Our altars are dry" (his field still heals him), collapses (a field on a falling section is lost), long fights.

## Against the apex (owner question 23 Nov 20:39; panel review; the three hunt rules CONFIRMED by the owner 20:40)
In the hunt there are no other suits or bots: only the Tiger Demon and its two minions. Every kit must still work there.
- **The apex is 兵家 (owner 24 Nov 09:44-09:5x).** Moves renamed from 《孙子兵法》 (其疾如风, 动如雷震, 侵掠如火，不动如山; minions 正 and 奇), mechanics unchanged. Passive **攻其无备**: +20% from the demon and minions while a suit's escape skill is on cooldown. Escape per kit: 儒 Q, 法 Q, 名 Q, 农 W (dashes); 墨 Q (wall); 阴阳 Q (step back); 道 W (flow); 纵横 and 杂 their W once built (a dash). Spend the escape well or pay for it.
- **墨甲 (works as is; may be the safest pick):** 非攻 is always on (the apex always attacks); the 距云梯 wall stops the demon's Pounce and the minions' dashes at the wall (dash length checks it); the 衡 lever flings minions fully but only shoves the demon a short way (size); 九距之 blocks nine demon hits. Watch in the hunt sweep: it may be too safe.
- **纵横甲 (two hunt rules):** 合纵 links the demon and a minion, so hits on the demon also hurt the minion (a trade: a broken minion still sends its plate and skills home). 连横 swaps with a demon or minion to escape. Confirmed: **不攻自破** turns the apex units on each other: inside the circle the demon and minions attack each other for its length, and a minion broken by the demon still sends its plate home. Confirmed: **连横 · 事一强** in the hunt makes a minion that sees him wait about 1 s before its next skill (once per 6 s per minion), since there are no bots to steer.
- **杂甲 (one hunt rule):** 兼收并蓄 stores the demon's moves; **一字千金** casts the demon's own Pounce or Roar back at it at full strength. 兼儒墨's 墨 slow always applies (the apex always attacks), except in the demon's frenzy. Confirmed: **兼儒墨's 正名 on the demon seals its stolen skills** (the ones minion plates brought home) for about 2 s; on a minion it seals one skill. 合名法's exposure matters little in the hunt; its blink is the useful part.
- Check for all nine: the hunt bot sweep (test 42) is run per suit once kits exist; no suit may make the apex trivial or impossible.
